About the film Letterboxd

The film

Shot over two and a half weeks in western Massachusetts while living in campsites and a train museum, Yesterday, Nothing In My Life Changed explores characters’ messy emotions: it creates a world where people are unsure of their own emotions and even more unsure of how to express and deal with them.
